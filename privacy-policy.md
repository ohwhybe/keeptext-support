Privacy Policy — KeepText

Last updated: July 2026

KeepText is built so there is nothing to collect. There is no KeepText account,
no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not
run a server that receives your data, and we cannot read your snippets.

WHERE YOUR SNIPPETS ARE STORED

Your snippets are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they
also sync across your own Apple devices through your private iCloud account
(Apple's CloudKit). That sync happens directly between your devices and Apple's
iCloud — it does not pass through any server operated by us, and we have no
access to it. Apple's iCloud and its privacy terms govern that storage. If you
are not signed in to iCloud, your snippets simply stay on the one device.

THE KEEPTEXT KEYBOARD AND "FULL ACCESS"

KeepText includes an optional custom keyboard. iOS only lets a keyboard read the
snippets you've saved if you grant it "Full Access," so KeepText's keyboard asks
for it for that single purpose: to show you your own saved snippets. The keyboard
does not perform any network activity. It does not log, store, or transmit what
you type. Because KeepText has no server and no account, there is nowhere for
keystrokes to be sent even in principle.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE

Only your own snippets, and only to your own iCloud, and only if you're signed in
to iCloud. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS

You can export everything you've saved to a plain JSON file at any time from the
app's Settings, and you can delete snippets at any time.
