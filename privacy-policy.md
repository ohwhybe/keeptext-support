Privacy Policy — KeepText

Last updated: May 2026

KeepText does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

All text snippets are saved locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults 
and are never sent to any server or third party.

KeepText does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
No account or sign-in is required to use the app.
