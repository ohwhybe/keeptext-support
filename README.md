## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I save text?**
Select any text in any app → tap Share → choose KeepText. 
It saves instantly.

**How do I copy a saved snippet?**
Open KeepText and tap any snippet. It copies to your clipboard immediately.

**Where is my data stored?**
Everything stays on your device. KeepText does not collect or transmit any data.

**The app isn't showing in my Share Sheet — what do I do?**
Go to Share → scroll to the bottom → tap "Edit Actions" → 
find KeepText and enable it.
