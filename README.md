# KeepText

Save text once. Paste it anywhere — including inside any app, with the KeepText keyboard.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I save text?**
Three ways:
- Select any text in any app → tap Share → choose KeepText.
- Copy something, then open KeepText — it offers to save it.
- Type it straight into the search field; if nothing matches, that field creates the snippet.

**How do I use a snippet inside another app, without switching apps?**
Use the KeepText keyboard. Switch to it inside Mail, Messages, or any app, and
tap a snippet to insert it right at your cursor. To enable it once:
Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → KeepText, then tap
KeepText in that list and turn on **Allow Full Access**.

**Why does the keyboard need "Full Access"? Is it safe?**
iOS only lets a keyboard read your saved snippets if you grant Full Access, so
that's the only reason KeepText asks. The keyboard sends nothing anywhere — it
has no network access, and KeepText has no server or account to send anything to.
It only reads the snippets you saved, to show them to you.

**How do I copy a saved snippet?**
Open KeepText and tap any snippet — it copies to your clipboard immediately.
You can also add a KeepText widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, or find a
snippet through Spotlight (swipe down and search).

**Do my snippets sync between my devices?**
Yes, through your own iCloud account, automatically. There's no KeepText account —
it uses your private iCloud. If you're not signed in to iCloud, snippets stay on
the one device.

**What are templates?**
Put `{{name}}` or `{{date}}` (any word in double braces) in a snippet. When you
use it, KeepText asks you for those values and fills them in.

**Is there a limit?**
The free version keeps up to 20 snippets. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks
unlimited snippets — no subscription.

**The app isn't showing in my Share Sheet — what do I do?**
Tap Share in any app → scroll to the bottom of the app row → tap "More" (or
"Edit Actions") → find KeepText and enable it.

**How do I get my data out?**
Settings → export gives you a plain JSON file of everything you've saved.
